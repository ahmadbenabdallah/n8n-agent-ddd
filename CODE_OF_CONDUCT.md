# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in n8n Agent DDD respectful, constructive and professional.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experiences and technical perspectives.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- be respectful and professional;
- discuss technical disagreements in good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, implementations and evidence;
- welcome constructive feedback;
- respect project boundaries and security requirements;
- help maintain a safe environment for contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or discrimination;
- threats or intimidation;
- personal attacks;
- deliberate disruption;
- publishing private information without permission;
- attempting to bypass project security controls;
- maliciously exposing secrets or customer data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to violations, including warnings, moderation, removal of content or restriction of participation.

Security-related conduct may also be handled under `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project's designated maintainers or project contact rather than escalating through public arguments.
