# Source of Truth

### KB
Useful for:
- explaining campaigns
- announcing known promotion codes
- describing human-readable terms

Not authoritative for:
- current eligibility
- remaining usage
- expiry
- customer restriction
- exact discount
- final cart discount

### WooCommerce
Authoritative for transactional coupon properties and validation.
