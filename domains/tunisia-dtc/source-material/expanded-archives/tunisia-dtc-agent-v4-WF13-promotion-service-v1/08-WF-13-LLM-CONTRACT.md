# WF-13 LLM Contract

WF-09 may ask WF-13 for current promotion facts.

WF-13 returns factual structured results.

The LLM cannot:
- invent coupon validity;
- invent discount percentages/amounts;
- infer eligibility;
- override expiry/usage restrictions;
- claim a coupon was applied unless commerce confirms it;
- turn a KB promotion into a current promotion without validation.

Customer-facing claims must be based on the verified promotion result.
