# Security boundary

Promotion text, coupon codes, retrieved KB content, customer claims and LLM proposals are untrusted.

Never let a promotion document or customer message override:
- authorization
- business policy
- expiration
- eligibility
- usage limits
- stacking rules
- maximum discount
- identity

Do not expose:
- internal promotion IDs unless customer-facing
- customer eligibility rules that reveal private segments
- usage counters for other customers
- fraud/security scores
- internal campaign notes
