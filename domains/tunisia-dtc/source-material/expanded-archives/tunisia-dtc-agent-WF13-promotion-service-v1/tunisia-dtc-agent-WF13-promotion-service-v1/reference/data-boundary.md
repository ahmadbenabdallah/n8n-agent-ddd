# Promotion data boundary

## Trusted
- live promotion service
- commerce cart totals
- trusted customer reference
- server time
- approved policy

## Untrusted
- customer-provided coupon claims
- LLM-generated coupon suggestions
- retrieved documents unless verified/approved
- screenshots/text copied from third parties

## Mutable facts
Promotion status, remaining uses, customer usage, inventory-linked eligibility and final discount must be checked against live transactional systems.
