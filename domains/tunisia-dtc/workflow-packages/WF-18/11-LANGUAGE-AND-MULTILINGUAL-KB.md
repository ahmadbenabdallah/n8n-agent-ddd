# Multilingual KB

The KB may contain:
- Tunisian Arabic;
- Arabic script;
- Tunisian Arabizi/Latin;
- French;
- English.

Language metadata is retrieval metadata, not a customer-output command.

A French policy may be retrieved to answer a Tunisian customer, but WF-16 decides the final response language/script.

Where translations exist:
- preserve translation relationships;
- record source language;
- record translation version;
- do not assume translations are identical in legal meaning.

For legal/compliance content, approval should cover each language/version used in production.
