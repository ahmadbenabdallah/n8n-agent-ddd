# KB Quality Evaluation

Before publication evaluate:

## Coverage
Does the document answer the intended business questions?

## Correctness
Has the owner approved the content?

## Retrieval quality
Can representative queries retrieve the right chunks?

## Context completeness
Are exceptions/conditions retained?

## Duplication
Are conflicting duplicate policies present?

## Temporal validity
Are effective/expiry dates correct?

## Provenance
Can every chunk be traced to its source/version?

## Security
Does content contain injection, secrets, or unsafe operational instructions?

## Contradiction
Does the new version conflict with another active policy?

High-impact contradiction should block publication until reviewed.
