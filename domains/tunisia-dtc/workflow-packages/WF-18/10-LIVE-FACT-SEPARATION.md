# Stable KB vs Live Commerce Facts

## KB can answer

- What is the return policy?
- What sizes are generally available?
- What materials/specifications are documented?
- What payment methods are supported?
- What is the published shipping policy?

## Live systems must answer

- Is size 42 in stock right now?
- What is the current price?
- Is this coupon valid for this cart/customer?
- What is the current shipping fee?
- What is the checkout total?
- What is the order status?
- Was the payment completed?

The KB may contain historical or default values, but those must not be presented as current live facts when a live source exists.
