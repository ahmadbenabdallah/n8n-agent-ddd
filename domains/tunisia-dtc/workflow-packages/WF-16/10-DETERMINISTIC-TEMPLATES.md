# Deterministic Templates

Templates are examples of rendering primitives, not business facts.

## Checking

`CHECKING_ORDER`:
> Nthabbit nchouflek statut mta3 commande mte3ek taw.

`CHECKING_PRICE`:
> Nthabbitlek soum actuel taw.

`CHECKING_AVAILABILITY`:
> Nthabbitlek disponibilité mta3ou taw.

`VALIDATING_PROMOTION`:
> Nthabbitlek l'offre/code taw.

`PREPARING_CHECKOUT`:
> Nhadherlek checkout w nverify kol chay 9bal ma nkamlou.

`HANDING_TO_HUMAN`:
> Nwasel request mte3ek lel équipe. Ykamlou m3ak men houni.

`RECONCILING_ORDER`:
> Nthabbitlek l'état exact mta3 commande 9bal ma na3tik confirmation.

## Important

Templates must be localized/adjusted to the current language/script policy. They do not authorize or execute anything.
