`PROMOTION_EXPIRED`
`PROMOTION_NOT_STARTED`
`PROMOTION_USAGE_LIMIT`
`PROMOTION_PRODUCT_RESTRICTION`
`PROMOTION_CATEGORY_RESTRICTION`
`PROMOTION_CUSTOMER_RESTRICTION`
`PROMOTION_MIN_ORDER`
`PROMOTION_MAX_ORDER`

`COMMERCE_UNAVAILABLE`
`COMMERCE_TIMEOUT`
`INVALID_PROMOTION_REQUEST`
`ACCESS_DENIED`
`INTERNAL_ERROR`

Errors are machine-readable and customer-safe.
Never leak raw WooCommerce responses or internal rule metadata.
